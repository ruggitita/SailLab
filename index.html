How to read the shifts

The current direction is estimated from the bisector between the average courses of port and starboard upwind legs. A clockwise change is shown as a right shift; a counter-clockwise change as a left shift. GPS course over ground is affected by current, leeway, waves and poor GPS fixes, so use steady legs and compare repeated samples. This is an estimate, not a replacement for a wind sensor.
