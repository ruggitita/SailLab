1. Straight-line analysis

Stable-course segments are inferred from GPS samples. Compare speed, progress, sailing angle and consistency. Wind-based metrics require a wind direction in Session details.

Stability values describe variation, not a score; compare similar conditions and treat inferred wind and course values cautiously.

Entry, minimum and exit speed; VMG and distance through the turn; turn angle, rotation rate, heel, deceleration and recovery acceleration.
